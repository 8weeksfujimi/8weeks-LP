3. 利用目的

  • ご予約の受付、空き状況の確認、ご予約についてのご連絡

4. 第三者への提供

法令に基づく場合を除き、ご本人の同意なく第三者に提供しません。旅館業法により、都道府県知事から宿泊者名簿の提出を求められたときは、提出します。

5. 利用しているサービスと、国外での取扱い

  • 予約システム: Beds24（運営は Beds24 GmbH・ドイツ）。予約ページでご入力いただいた情報は、欧州連合（EU）・欧州経済領域（EEA）の中で処理され、ドイツにあるサーバーに保管されます。
  • 決済: Stripe。お支払いの処理にともない、米国などへ個人データが移されることがあります。契約の相手はストライプジャパン株式会社です。

6. 保存の期間

  • 宿泊者名簿（お名前、ご住所、ご連絡先。日本国内に住所のない外国籍の方は、国籍と旅券番号）は、旅館業法施行規則により、作成の日から3年間保存します。
  • 3年を過ぎた宿泊者名簿は消去します。お問い合わせのメールなど、そのほかの情報は、対応が終わってから3年を目安に消去します。

7. 安全管理のために講じている措置

  • 個人情報を取り扱うのは、代表者と運営の担当者に限ります。
  • 端末とアカウントは、パスワードと2段階認証で守ります。

2. Information we collect

When you send a booking request (the fields on the booking page)

  • Title, name, email address, mobile phone number, address, country of residence
  • Optional: phone number, postal code, expected arrival time, comments or requests
  • For foreign nationals without an address in Japan: nationality and passport number
  • Details of your booking (property, dates, number of guests, price)

When you contact us by email

  • Your name, email address and the content of your message

When you pay

  • Payment is made through the payment service Stripe. Card details such as your card number are received directly by Stripe; we do not receive or store them.

3. How we use it

  • To receive booking requests, check availability, and contact you about your booking
  • To provide your stay, and to contact you before and after it
  • To create and keep the guest register required by Japan's Inns and Hotels Act (Article 6)
  • To request and confirm payment, and to make refunds
  • To reply to your enquiries

We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law. Under the Inns and Hotels Act, we must submit the guest register when the prefectural governor requests it.

5. Services we use, and handling outside Japan

  • Booking system: Beds24 (operated by Beds24 GmbH, Germany). The information you enter on the booking page is processed within the European Union (EU) and the European Economic Area (EEA), and stored on servers in Germany.
  • Payments: Stripe. In processing payments, personal data may be transferred to the United States and other countries. Our contract is with Stripe Japan, Inc.

6. How long we keep it

  • The guest register (name, address and contact details; for foreign nationals without an address in Japan, also nationality and passport number) is kept for three years from the date it is made, as required by the Enforcement Regulations of the Inns and Hotels Act.
  • Guest register records are deleted once the three years have passed. Other information, such as inquiry emails, is deleted about three years after the matter is closed.

7. Security measures

  • Personal information is handled only by our representative and the person in charge of operations.
  • Devices and accounts are protected with passwords and two-step verification.

8. Your requests, and how to contact us

If you would like to be told the purposes for which we use your personal information, or to have it disclosed, corrected, suspended from use or deleted, or if you have a question or complaint about how we handle it, please contact us below. We will respond after confirming your identity.
