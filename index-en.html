Frequently Asked Questions

Getting here, the three stays, and how booking works

Where is 8weeks Stay?
In Fujimi-machi (Fujimi town), Nagano Prefecture, Japan: a highland town at about 1,000 m elevation at the foot of the Yatsugatake mountains, about two hours from Tokyo. This is Fujimi in Nagano, not the city of Fujimi in Saitama.
How do I get there from Tokyo?
It takes about two hours, by car or by JR Chuo Line limited express to Fujimi Station. 8weeks Studio is a 3-minute walk from the station. For 8weeks Fujimi and 8weeks Quriu, a car is the easiest way to get around. All three stays have free parking.
What are the three stays like?
8weeks Fujimi is a private house in the forest with a grand piano. 8weeks Quriu is a private floor of more than 100 m² with mountain views and a wood stove, with room for two families. 8weeks Studio is a renovated flat on Fujimi's shopping street, a 3-minute walk from the station. Each has two bedrooms and a kitchen.
How do I book, and is it cheaper to book direct?
Choose a stay and press “Book direct” to open our booking page, where you can see the calendar and the price for your dates and send a booking request. Booking direct costs about 5% less than booking the same stay on Airbnb. Stays of 7 nights or more, and of 28 nights or more, are discounted automatically. You can also book on Airbnb if you prefer.
What happens after I send a booking request?
We check availability and reply by email within 48 hours. Nothing is charged when you send the request. Payment is by credit card.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation is free until 5 days before check-in. For stays of 28 nights or more, it is free until 30 days before check-in. The full policy is shown on the booking page.
How does check-in work?
All three stays are self check-in: a key box at 8weeks Fujimi and 8weeks Quriu, from 3 pm, and a smart lock with a door code at 8weeks Studio.
Is there parking, and can you store luggage?
All three stays have free parking. All three can also keep your luggage before check-in or after check-out. Please ask your host after booking.
Can I ski or go mountain biking nearby?
Yes. Fujimi Panorama Resort is about 3 minutes by car from 8weeks Fujimi and about 5 minutes from 8weeks Quriu. From Fujimi Station, a 3-minute walk from 8weeks Studio, a free shuttle bus runs to the resort during its operating season; please check the resort's official site for dates and times. Winter is for skiing and snowboarding, and from spring to autumn there are the gondola, the mountain-bike park and trekking.
