8 Weeks to Call This Place Home

In 2020, we moved from the city with our one-year-old daughter and fell in love with Fujimi-machi, deciding to settle here within just 8 weeks.

After spending luxurious time surrounded by rich nature, we found ourselves thinking "I want to keep living in this town."

We named our retreat "8weeks" from our wish that every guest would feel the same — "I want to stay here."

Day Trips to Surrounding Areas

Hara Village · Tateshina 20-30 min
  • Tateshina Barakura English Garden — Tea time in an authentic English garden
  • Yatsugatake Alpaca Farm — Interactive experiences with adorable alpacas
  • Hara Village Star Festival — One of Japan's best stargazing spots
  • Kurumayama Kogen — Wildflowers in summer, skiing in winter
Suwa 30 min
  • Suwa Taisha Shrines — One of Japan's oldest shrine complexes
  • Lake Suwa — Lakeside walks and hot spring town exploration
  • Katakura-kan — Showa retro public bath experience
Kobuchizawa 30 min
  • Highland cafe hopping — Hidden gems in southern Yatsugatake foothills
  • Risonare Kobuchizawa — Elegant highland resort
  • Local craft shops — Discover works by local artisans
Hakushu · Nirasaki 30-40 min
  • Suntory Hakushu Distillery — Whisky factory tour in the forest
  • Ojira River Valley — Crystal clear streams and mystical waterfalls
  • Chateraise Hakushu Factory — Ice cream factory tour and tasting
  • Shosenkyo Gorge — One of Japan's premier gorge landscapes
